My Vision for Ward 8

Together, we can build a Ward 8 where everyone feels safe, valued, and at home; whether you’re a homeowner, student, family, or newcomer.

Affordable Housing & Inclusive Growth

Supporting gentle density solutions like duplexes and townhomes, protecting existing affordable units, and partnering with organizations to make housing attainable without displacing families or changing our neighbourhood’s character.

Vacant Property Tax & Tenant Protections

Advocating for a vacant property tax to discourage speculation and empty homes, and pushing for a maximum heat bylaw to ensure tenants have safe, comfortable housing year‑round.

A Councillor Who Listens & Acts

Committing to regular town halls, transparent sharing of council votes, and empowering residents to shape budget priorities through participatory tools.

Transit & LRT Readiness

Advocating for better bus routes, increased weekend service, and public education so Ward 8 residents can confidently connect with the upcoming LRT and reduce car dependency.

Road Safety & Traffic Calming

Tackling speeding and truck traffic in residential areas with expanded speed humps, traffic‑calming zones, and improved intersections like Claremont Access to protect families and seniors.

Aging Infrastructure & Safer Streets

Championing complete‑streets upgrades with sidewalks, bike lanes, and accessibility improvements on Upper James, Fennell, and Rymal Road; because safer, walkable neighbourhoods improve quality of life for all ages.

Stronger Union Protections & Workers’ Rights

Supporting local unions and workers by fighting for fair wages, safe workplaces, and the right to organize; building a stronger economy that works for all.

Community Safety & Stronger Partnerships

Working closely with Hamilton Police and community organizations to reduce crime, increase community engagement, and support intervention programs that keep our neighbourhoods safe and welcoming.

Climate Action & Green Initiatives

Fighting for expanded tree planting, city fleet electrification, and green development policies that make Ward 8 a leader in sustainability and resilience.

Parks, Trails & Recreation for All

Ensuring equitable access to splash pads, trail connectivity along the mountain brow, and safe, accessible green spaces where kids, families, and seniors can gather and thrive.

Arts, Music & Culture

Backing local artists, music festivals, and grassroots cultural groups with sustainable funding, inclusive event spaces, and ward‑level arts grants that reflect the vibrancy of our communities.

Healthcare Supports & Education Resources

Championing better local healthcare access, mental health supports, and educational programs that empower families, youth, and seniors to thrive.
