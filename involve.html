Ways You Can Join the Campaign

🚪 Volunteer

From door-knocking and phone banking to data entry and event setup, we’ve got a place for you! No experience needed, just energy, heart, and commitment to improving Ward 8.

📬 Request a Lawn Sign

Let your neighbours know you support a people-powered campaign! We’ll drop one off when signs are released closer to voting day.

🏠 Host a Community Chat

Bring friends, neighbours, or family together to meet Alex and talk about local priorities! We’ll bring materials and answers, you provide the space and company.

💸 Donate

Help us print flyers, run outreach, and mobilize volunteers. Every dollar goes directly to reaching voters and changing the conversation at City Hall.

📣 Spread the Word

Share our posts, talk to your neighbours, or invite a friend to volunteer. Grassroots change happens one conversation at a time.

📝 Policy Feedback

Your voice matters. If you have ideas for Ward 8 or want to co-develop policy proposals, email us or drop into one of our town halls.
